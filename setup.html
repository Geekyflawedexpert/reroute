Reroute · setup

Make Instagram come here first

Reroute can’t block anything, and it doesn’t try to. It just needs your phone to send you here when you reach for Instagram — your phone’s own automation does that.

Your link

Before anything else

Add Reroute to your home screen, and open it from that icon rather than the browser. On iPhone that’s Share → Add to Home Screen.

This isn’t cosmetic. Installed, the app runs standalone and your log is exempt from Safari clearing storage for sites unused over a week — without it you can lose three weeks of data.

What it won’t do

It fires when Instagram is launched, but not reliably when you return to it from the app switcher, and it’s bypassed if you open Instagram from a notification or a link. That’s the ceiling of what any web app can do — real interception needs a native build.
